If you have a pile of Pokémon cards at home and a player who isn't quite ready for the full trading card game, this one is for you.

I first made these rules for my son's birthday party. We wanted every guest to be able to jump in and battle — little siblings, grandparents, friends who had never held a card before. The official game is amazing, but it has a lot to learn. So we stripped it down to the parts that make it fun: picking your favorite Pokémon, powering it up, and cheering when the big number wins.

All Trainers Battle is built for every age and every ability. It works for young kids, for older players with learning or physical disabilities, and for grown-ups who just want an easy way in. There are three levels, so the same deck grows right along with the player — all the way to the real game.

What you need

Players, cards & a table

  • 2 or more players, any age. Little ones can play with a helper.
  • Pokémon cards — any cards with an HP number work.
  • Energy cards — the more, the merrier.
  • Trainer cards (optional) — no reading required, we'll show you how.
  • For Level 2: a few dice, coins, or a pencil and paper to track damage.
Before you play

Get to know your card

1 HP
The big number at the top right. It shows how strong your Pokémon is.
2 Type symbol
The small symbol by the HP — a flame, a water drop, a leaf, and more. Match it with Energy for a bigger boost!
3 Attacks
Listed in the middle. The number on the right is the damage. You'll use these in Level 2.
4 Weakness
Near the bottom. If an attacker's type shows here, its attacks hit twice as hard (Level 2).
Set up

  1. Pokémon stack: each player picks 5, 10, or as many Pokémon as they like.
  2. Energy pile: each player sets aside about half as many Energy cards as Pokémon. More is totally fine!
  3. Trainer cards (optional): add 1 Trainer card for every 5 Pokémon.
  4. Choose or Surprise? In Choose, players look at their stack and pick each battle Pokémon. In Surprise, shuffle and flip the top card — faster, and no decisions needed.
Level 1 · Start here

HP Showdown

Perfect for first-time players, pre-readers, and anyone who loves a big number.

  1. Battle! Everyone puts one Pokémon face up at the same time.
  2. Power up (optional): add 1 Energy card from your pile.
  3. Trainer boost (optional): play a Trainer card. Each Trainer card is used once.
  4. Add it up. The highest total HP wins the round! A tie? Everyone high-fives.
  5. Clear the table. Used cards go into your own "done" pile. Everyone keeps their own cards.

Level 2 · Ready for more?

Attack Battle

This is how the real game works, made simpler. Pokémon trade attacks until one runs out of HP.

  1. Battle spot: each player puts one Pokémon in front of them.
  2. Take turns. On your turn, attach 1 Energy to your Pokémon. Energy stays attached for the rest of the battle.
  3. Attack! Pick one of your Pokémon's attacks and do that much damage. Skip any extra words on the attack. No number? It does 10 damage.
  4. Track damage. Subtract the damage from the other Pokémon's HP. Dice, coins, or paper all work.
  5. Knocked out! When a Pokémon's HP hits 0, it's knocked out and its Trainer brings out the next Pokémon.
Level 2 bonuses: Attacks do +10 damage if the Pokémon has matching Energy attached. And if the defending Pokémon shows the attacker's type next to Weakness, the attack does double damage.
Winning: Knock out 3 Pokémon (Quick) or all of the other player's Pokémon (Classic and Marathon).
Level 3 · The real deal

Ready for the real game

When Level 2 feels easy, your Trainer is ready for the official trading card game. The big new ideas are:

  • Attack costs: each attack needs the right Energy attached before it can be used.
  • The Bench: up to five Pokémon wait on the sidelines, ready to jump in.
  • Prize cards: take a Prize card each time you knock out a Pokémon. Take them all to win.
  • Evolution: grow your Pokémon into stronger forms during the game.

A two-player starter box with ready-made decks and a guided first game is the easiest next step (see our picks below). You can also find the official rules on Pokemon.com, and many local game stores run free beginner leagues.

Mix it up

Ways to play

🤝

Team Up

Everyone works together against one big "Boss" Pokémon picked by a grown-up. Add up the team's damage each turn until the Boss is knocked out. Nobody loses!

🎨

Favorite Color Bonus

If your Pokémon is your favorite color, it gets +30 HP. Tell everyone your favorite color before you start.

👀

No-Reading Mode

Just match symbols and compare numbers. A helper can point to the HP and count along. Energy matching becomes a color-and-shape match game.

✋

Helping Hands

A card stand, a playmat, or sleeved cards make holding and flipping easier. Players can also point to their choice and let a helper place the card.

For grown-ups & helpers

Tips to make it magic

  • Use big battle voices and silly sound effects.
  • Ask, "Why do you think your Pokémon is strong?" It builds storytelling and confidence.
  • Let players make up their own attack names.
  • Celebrate great Energy matches and smart choices, not just wins.
  • Keep favorite cards safe in sleeves so nobody worries about bent corners.
