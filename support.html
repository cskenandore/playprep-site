Common Questions

Quick answers

Most questions can be answered right here — no need to wait on an email.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account & data. You'll confirm with your password, and your account is scheduled for deletion 24 hours later — you can cancel anytime before then from the same screen or the email we send you.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not inside PlayPrep directly — that's how Apple and Google require it to work. Cancelling there stops future renewals; you'll keep access until the current period ends.

How many devices can I use PlayPrep on?

Pro and Pro Plus include cloud sync across up to 5 devices, including the web version. You can view and manage your connected devices anytime from Settings → Privacy & Data → Manage Devices.

Is my data private?

Yes — we don't sell your data, and it's never shared with advertisers. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Send us a message using the form below with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.

I have an idea for a new feature

We'd love to hear it. Use our feedback form for feature ideas and general thoughts — it helps us keep track of what people actually want.
